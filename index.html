FAQ

How do I get a key?

Click Get Key at the top of this page, complete the checkpoints on the page you land on, then copy the key you receive.

How long does a key last?

Depends on the key system. When your key expires, just come back here and get a new one.

It doesn't work, what can I do?

Make sure you pasted the full key with no extra spaces, and that your executor is up to date. If it still fails, get a new key and try again.

Is it free?

Yes, getting a key is free. You only need to complete the checkpoints.
